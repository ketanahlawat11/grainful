The Premium Option for Best Cuisine

Basmati Rice

Famous for its thin, long grains and unique fragrance, basmati rice is essential for gourmet dishes in kitchens around the world.

Its name, meaning “fragrance” in Hindi, perfectly expresses the essence of this prized rice variety. At Grainful, our basmati rice is meticulously aged to bring out its signature fragrance, fluffiness, and non-sticky texture. Hence, making it an excellent choice for culinary professionals. We ensure providing high-quality products suitable for supermarkets, hotels, and large-scale caterers and retail markets.

Key Features of Our Basmati Rice:

  • Aged to Perfection

    To optimize the flavour and texture of our Basmati rice is aged for up to 12 months, ensuring each grain remains separate and fragrant during cooking.

  • Nutrient-Rich and Reasonably Priced

    Our long, thin grains that expand during cooking are ideal for restaurant-caliber presentation.

  • Aromatic Excellence

    Naturally fragrant, turning every meal into a gourmet pleasure.

Non-Basmati Rice

Non-basmati rice, though less fragrant than Basmati, is a staple in millions worldwide due to its versatility and cost-efficiency.

Grainful offers a broad selection of non-Basmati rice, including short-grain, and medium-grain, meeting the demands of diverse consumers, from everyday meals to specialized dishes.

Key Features of Our Non-Basmati Rice:

  • Variety of Grains

    Options for different applications, from everyday meals to specialized culinary needs.

  • Nutrient-Rich and Reasonably Priced

    Retaining vital nutrients, hence making it a healthy and affordable option for bulk buyers.

  • Consistent Quality

    Stringent quality controls, guaranteeing a consistent flavour and reliability in every shipment.
