Storage & Usage Instructions

To maintain the purity, freshness and quality of your ghee

  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
  • Always use a clean, dry spoon.
  • Do not use a wet spoon, as moisture may affect product quality.
  • Keep away from heat sources such as gas stoves, ovens and other hot appliances.
  • Close the lid tightly after every use to preserve freshness and aroma.
  • Do not mix this ghee with ghee from another container or brand.
  • Always keep the jar clean and dry.
  • Refrigeration is not required.
